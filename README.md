## PATENT 1: Automatic Side Mirror Folding System Using Ultrasonic Sensor

**Application Number:** 202511104181 A

**Country:** India

**Filing Date:** 29 October 2025

**Publication Date:** 12 December 2025

**Applicant:** Lovely Professional University

**Inventors:** Akash Gupta, Alif Md Khairul Arshi, Sumit Kanchan

### Description

This invention presents an automatic vehicle side mirror folding system using ultrasonic sensors and a microcontroller.

The system detects nearby obstacles and automatically folds the vehicle's side mirrors when an obstacle comes within a predefined distance. It is designed to prevent mirror damage in narrow roads, parking areas, and crowded spaces.

**Key Features:**
- Ultrasonic obstacle detection.
- Automatic mirror folding.
- Microcontroller-based operation.
- Vehicle mirror protection.

## PATENT 2: Digitally Controlled Emergency Vehicle Starter Device

**Application Number:** 202511104167 A

**Country:** India

**Filing Date:** 29 October 2025

**Publication Date:** 12 December 2025

**Applicant:** Lovely Professional University

**Inventors:** Akash Gupta, Alif Md Khairul Arshi, Dr. Manjeet Singh

### Description

This invention introduces a digitally controlled emergency vehicle starter device designed to assist in starting vehicles with weak batteries without requiring another vehicle.

The system uses a regulated power supply, microcontroller, voltage and current sensors, and electronic protection mechanisms to provide controlled power output.

**Key Features:**
- Digitally controlled power regulation.
- Voltage and current monitoring.
- Temperature monitoring and protection.
- User interface with display and control buttons.
- Emergency vehicle starting assistance.

## PATENT 3: Vehicle Traffic Light Detection and Driver Alert System

**Application Number:** 202511104183 A

**Country:** India

**Filing Date:** 29 October 2025

**Publication Date:** 12 December 2025

**Applicant:** Lovely Professional University

**Inventors:** Akash Gupta, Alif Md Khairul Arshi, Sumit Kanchan

### Description

This invention presents a vehicle traffic light detection and driver alert system using an ESP32-CAM module.

The system captures and processes roadway images to detect traffic signal conditions. When a yellow traffic signal is detected, it activates audible and visual alerts to inform the driver.

**Key Features:**
- Camera-based traffic signal detection.
- ESP32-CAM image processing.
- Audible driver alerts.
- Visual traffic signal indication.
- Electronic control system.

# RESEARCH PUBLICATIONS

## Research Paper 1: A Comprehensive Review on Utilization of Agricultural Waste for Reinforced Structural Products: A Sustainable Perspective

**Authors:** M. Singh, N. Kumar, A. Gupta, I. Palai, A. Kumari, A. MD Kh. Arshi

**Journal:** Bulgarian Chemical Communications

**Publication Year:** 2025

**Volume:** 57, Special Issue B

**Pages:** 05–16

**DOI:** 10.34049/bcc.57.B.A0001

### Abstract and Overview

This review paper explores the sustainable utilization of agricultural waste for developing reinforced structural products and environmentally friendly construction materials.

It examines different sources of agricultural waste, including crop residues, agricultural by-products, field residues, processing residues, and industrial residues, and their potential applications in structural materials.

The paper discusses various processing techniques, mechanical and structural properties, environmental benefits, economic viability, and challenges associated with converting agricultural waste into valuable structural products.

### Key Research Areas

- Agricultural waste utilization and recycling.
- Natural fibre-reinforced composites.
- Sustainable structural and construction materials.
- Mechanical properties of agro-based materials.
- Environmental sustainability and waste management.
- Circular economy and resource efficiency.
